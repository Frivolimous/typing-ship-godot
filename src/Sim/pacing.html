Level pacing report

Millennium Typer Pacing

How fast each level asks you to type, second by second. Demand is the letters of every word that appears (ships, missiles, turrets, boss words) over a rolling 10-second window, as words per minute at 5 letters a word, or as a share of the difficulty's stated speed (100% = typing flat out). A perfect bot plays each level at the stated speed. Above it, words pile up on screen; below it, you get breathing room. Game levels are data/levels, Classic the original 12 (data/levels/classic): the Classic shares are the calibration the reworked levels should keep.
