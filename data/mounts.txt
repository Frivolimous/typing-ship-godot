# shield bubble around it.

#   sprite   x,y   flame   width   length   colour
#   sprite   x,y   charge
#   sprite   x,y   shield  width   height
#
#   sprite   the art's path under game/assets/sprites, without .png (ai/player, ai/stealth)
#   x,y      a point on that PNG, in its pixels, measured from its centre (x right, y down; the art points up)
#   width    how wide the flame is at the nozzle, in the same pixels
#   length   how long the flame is, in the same pixels (it flickers around this)
#   colour   blue, cyan, orange, red, purple or white
#   shield   the bubble's centre (x,y), its width across the ship and its height along it, in the art's pixels
#
# Art without flames has none; a ship without a charge point charges at its centre; a ship without a shield line keeps
# its hull's shield size (in View/Views.cs).
