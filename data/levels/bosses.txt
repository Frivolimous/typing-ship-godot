# The boss fights. How to write these: docs/level-format.md.
#
# [boss N escorts] are the ships sent in, over and over, while boss N is on screen (from where the ship is when the
# previous batch is used up). [boss 1 hangar] is the six suicide ships boss 1 launches from its hangar each attack:
# their positions are on the same 12 x 12 grid, but measured from the boss art's top-left corner, not the screen's.

[boss 1 escorts]
missile sn 0.3  0,4 7,6 12,6
missile sn 0.015  12,3 5,5 0,5
repeat 0.015 2 2

laser ss 0.2  0,9
mirror 0.01

[boss 1 hangar]
ship msn 0  1.6,3  halt 16  attack
ship msn 0  2.2,3.4  halt 13  attack
ship msn 0  2.8,3.7  halt 10  attack
ship msn 0  6.8,3.7  halt 10  attack
ship msn 0  7.4,3.4  halt 13  attack
ship msn 0  8,3  halt 16  attack

[boss 2 escorts]
missile mn 0.25  0,3~6 5,6 12,7
mirror
repeat 0.03 1 2

laser ms 0.35  0,8
mirror 0.01

[boss 3 escorts]
laser ms 0.3  0,6~9
mirror
missile mn 0.3  0,0 2,4 5,6 12,10
repeat 0.02 5 1 mirrored
stealth 0.3  2~10,5~7 0|12,5
